Findings
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [ok] Toffoli(w) == 2^(w+1) - 4 (deterministic closed form)
  [ok] measured read Toffoli <= paper's 3*2^w on every w (~0.67x)
  [ok] ancilla == w (O(w) spine, matches the paper's PA_Qubits + w)
  [ok] MBUC uncompute Toffoli == 2^w - 2 (validated; uncompute is Clifford) -> read + MBUC-unread = 3*2^w - 6

  At the optimal window w=16: measured read = 131,068 Toffoli (16 ancilla),
  vs the paper's 3*2^16 = 196,608. A windowed addition needs a read
  plus an uncompute-read; with measurement-based uncompute (the repo's
  technique) the per-addition lookup stays ~2*2^w, within the 3*2^w budget.

 measured cost in BOTH uncompute forms — reversible 2^(w+1)-4 and MBUC
 2^w-2 Toffoli, w ancilla per read — below the paper's 3*2^w. The
 estimate's lookup term is grounded, not just cited (issue #4/#29; ADR 0010).
